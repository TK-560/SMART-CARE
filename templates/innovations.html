Innovation Features

Prototype overview · how each feature improves UX

Seven additions shown in the prototype. Each one is built to remove a specific friction point found in the current SmartCare workflow.

☰ Patient Portal

UX gain: one search point for every patient record.

Lets staff find a patient by number, name, or phone and jump straight to history, booking, or check-in — no more digging through paper files.

Open Patient Portal → ✉ SMS Reminders

UX gain: fewer no-shows, less re-scheduling.

Upcoming appointments are listed with patient mobiles and a one-tap send. Patients get a text 24 hours before, so empty slots shrink and diaries stay full.

Open SMS Reminders → ☐ Appointment Calendar

UX gain: the day's load at a glance.

A month grid shows which days are busy. Selecting a day reveals its exact schedule, making capacity planning and rescheduling intuitive.

Open Calendar → ⌁ Reports Dashboard

UX gain: decisions backed by data.

Visual charts of appointments by specialisation, status, gender, and recent daily volume give managers insight without exporting spreadsheets.

Open Reports → ◒ AI Chat Assistant

UX gain: answers on demand for new staff.

A scripted assistant answers common questions about booking, check-in, reminders, and hours, cutting training time and repeated questions to human staff.

Open AI Assistant → ▦ QR Code Check-in

UX gain: zero-queue arrivals.

Each appointment gets a unique scannable code. Patients scan on arrival and staff confirm in one tap, so reception clears instantly and doctors stay on schedule.

Open QR Check-in → ◷ Medical History Screen

UX gain: context before the consultation.

A timeline of past visits, doctors, outcomes, allergies, and chronic conditions means clinicians walk in prepared and patients don't repeat their story.

How the login, registration, booking and dashboard improved too

• Login: password visibility toggle, Remember Me, and self-service forgot-password flow. • Registration: sections, in-field validation messages, and a stepper that guides the user. • Booking: visual doctor cards, a pickable calendar and time-slot pills, plus a live summary that shows the Confirmed status before submitting. • Dashboard: quick actions for every routine task and a notification panel that flags what needs attention today.
