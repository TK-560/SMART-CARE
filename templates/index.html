What SmartCare Does

Everything your practice needs

Four core modules, each built to keep your clinic running smoothly.

Register Patients

Capture patient demographics, SA ID numbers, contact details, and emergency contacts with validated forms.

Register a Patient → Manage Doctors

Add, edit, and remove doctor records with their specialisations, phone numbers, and email addresses.

Manage Doctors → Book Appointments

Schedule appointments by selecting a patient, doctor, date, and time with automatic status tracking.
